# Stickyy 1.1.1

## Wider Mac compatibility

- Stickyy now supports macOS 14 and later on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- Liquid Glass remains available on macOS 26 and later; earlier systems use
  Stickyy's original minimal material appearance.
- Fixed a packaging-signature mismatch that could stop the downloaded app from
  launching after installation.

## A clearer welcome

- The welcome tour now includes an interactive preview of the floating note.

## Distribution

- Downloads and in-app update archives are now served directly from the
  Stickyy website.
