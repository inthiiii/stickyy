# Stickyy 1.2.1

## Links that behave like links

- URLs inside a scratchpad now appear in blue with an underline.
- Click a detected URL to open it in the default browser.

## More context, still minimal

- Click **Stickyy** in the menu-bar scratchpad header to reveal the active note
  title.
- The title returns to **Stickyy** after five seconds or immediately when clicked
  again.

## Faster clipboard capture

- The newest clipboard item appears as a compact button in the bottom-right of
  the floating note.
- Click it to insert text or attach the copied image or file.
- The full Clipboard Pills shelf remains hidden from the floating note.

## A clearer first launch and every update

- New users reliably receive the interactive onboarding tour and welcome note.
- Existing users receive a dedicated **Latest update 1.2.1 and the features**
  scratchpad once after upgrading.
- The Drag Stickyy into Applications window now preserves its custom installer
  background and layout.
