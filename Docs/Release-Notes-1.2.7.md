# Stickyy 1.2.7

## More control without losing the minimal feel

- Turn on a live word or character count for either note window.
- Use the compact **B**, *I*, and underline controls in the menu-bar scratchpad
  and floating note. Formatting is saved with the note.
- Optionally pin important Clipboard Pills. Pinned items stay in place while
  the remaining pill slots continue to update, and clearing the shelf leaves
  pinned items untouched.
- The floating note now shows one compact clipboard icon and accepts copied
  text only.
- Long notes remain correctly aligned and scroll smoothly while you type.

These options are available in Stickyy Settings and default to the familiar,
minimal behavior until you enable them.
