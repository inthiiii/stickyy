# Stickyy Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 13, 2026**

Stickyy is designed to keep your thoughts on your Mac.

## Data collection

Stickyy does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not
include advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or a cloud service.

## Local data

Scratchpads and their metadata are stored locally in Stickyy's macOS app
container. Clipboard Pills are temporary and remain in memory only while the app
is running. Turning Clipboard History off clears that temporary history.

When you choose to paste an image or file, Stickyy stores the attachment inside
its local app container. When you explicitly use the macOS share menu, the
selected system sharing service receives the scratchpad text under that service's
own privacy terms.

## Permissions and network access

Stickyy uses the macOS App Sandbox and does not require network access for its
core functionality. It does not request access to your microphone, camera,
contacts, calendar, location, or accessibility data.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published with a new effective date in
the Stickyy repository and on the Stickyy website.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be opened as an issue in the
[Stickyy GitHub repository](https://github.com/inthiiii/stickyy/issues).
